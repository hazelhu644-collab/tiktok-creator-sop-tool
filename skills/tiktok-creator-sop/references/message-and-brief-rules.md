# Message and Brief Rules

## Message Output

Always provide:

1. English message first.
2. Chinese explanation below explaining the purpose and wording strategy.

## Tone

Write like a US brand communicating with a creator:

- Professional but warm.
- Clear and direct.
- Not too humble.
- Not overly excited.
- Not pushy.
- Not like a translated Chinese message.
- No excessive compliments.
- No begging language.

Avoid phrases like:

- Please please.
- We really really hope.
- You are amazing and perfect.
- We are begging you.
- Sorry to bother you so much.

Prefer phrasing such as:

- Thanks for the update.
- Just checking in.
- Could you focus more on...
- This helps viewers understand the product better.
- Please let us know your expected posting date.
- If you are no longer able to move forward, please let us know so we can update the campaign status.

## Channel Styles

### TikTok DM

Use 2 to 4 short, natural, conversational sentences.

### TikTok Shop Affiliate Message

Use 4 to 6 clear, professional sentences with collaboration context and a next action.

### Email

Use a structured message: greeting, short context, clear request, next step, closing.

### WhatsApp

Use a conversational message, slightly more detailed than TikTok DM.

## Script Angles

Every scenario carries several angles rather than one fixed template, so a
batch of creators does not receive byte-identical messages. Angle 1 is the
neutral default; the rest change the approach, not the facts.

Outreach angles available:

- First Outreach: standard introduction, content-fit opener, offer-first, content-angle proposal, minimal low-pressure.
- Re-engagement Outreach: new product for a past collaborator, performance-led, new-round notice, light check-in.
- No Reply Follow-up: standard, yes-or-no, added detail, deadline, friendly close-out.
- Sample Request Reminder: standard, concrete steps, shipping-batch window, ask what is blocking.

Later-stage angles cover sample confirmation, in-transit, logistics exceptions,
delivered follow-up, partial completion, revision, final follow-up, completion,
archive, light check-in, and shipping-address confirmation.

Pick a different angle when the previous message on the same angle got no
reply. Repeating the same wording twice reads as automation.

## Relationship Tiers

Tone is layered on top of the angle for outreach-stage messages only:

- 冷启动 (cold): no familiarity claims, explicit low pressure. Never open with a compliment you have not earned.
- 腰部达人 (mid-tier): one line acknowledging their content.
- 头部达人 (top-tier): acknowledgement plus explicit flexibility on schedule and style.
- 老合作达人 (returning): reference the previous collaboration.

Logistics, revision, and final-confirmation messages stay neutral at every
tier. Softening a "this video breaks the brief" message makes it unclear.

## Collaboration Models

A campaign runs on one of two models, set in campaign settings:

- `affiliate-link`: the creator posts a TikTok Shop product link and earns the platform affiliate commission on it. The brand ships the sample.
- `discount-code`: the creator gets a personal code, their audience saves money with it, and commission is attributed through the code.

Order method is separate from the model:

- `brand-ships`: we send the sample and chase the shipping address when it is missing.
- `creator-orders`: the creator places the order themselves with a code that covers the full cost. They must leave their TikTok handle on the order, then send the order number so we can match it and return tracking.

The self-order path adds three stages between "interested" and "shipped":
confirm quantity and timing, send the order link and code, then chase the order
number. Nothing ships until the order number exists.

Never state a commission rate, audience discount, attribution window, or
discount code that is not configured on the campaign. Blank fields are omitted
from the copy rather than guessed at.

## Creator Source

- `Outreach`: found and contacted by us. Cold-outreach wording applies.
- `TCM`: accepted the invitation on TikTok Creator Marketplace. They have already opted in, so introduce the details rather than pitching from scratch.

TCM creators frequently ask why the marketplace shows a specific fee. The answer
is that the amount matches the value of the products sent — TikTok requires a
number in the collaboration-fee field — and that commission on their code
applies on top of it. Do not treat that question as a rate negotiation.

## Email Subjects

Email is the only channel with a subject line. First outreach, re-engagement,
TCM follow-up, completion, and archive get a fresh subject. Every follow-up
stays in the existing thread: the composer shows a note instead of a subject,
because a new subject splits the conversation and reads as a fresh cold email.

## Compliance

Every full brief asks for an `#ad` or branded-content disclosure. This is
required of US creators under FTC endorsement guidance and is easy for a creator
to forget, so it belongs in the brief rather than in a side note. It can be
switched off per campaign for markets where it does not apply.

When a campaign sets a content-usage window, the brief states it plainly: the
brand may reuse the content on its own channels — website, social, paid ads,
email — for that number of months.

## Commercial Questions

Answer these directly instead of deflecting to "I'll note that on our side":

- Commission or "is this paid?": sample is free and kept, standard affiliate commission on link sales, no separate posting fee.
- A quoted flat rate: state that this campaign is sample plus commission, then leave the door open for budgeted campaigns.
- A decline: thank them, close it out, stop following up.
- "Too busy right now": offer to pause and ask for a window rather than following up again.
- A request for another unit: ask which variant and which address, then confirm availability before promising.

## Product Categories

The brief and the English wording come from a category preset (pet, beauty,
home, kitchen, apparel, electronics, baby, food, fitness, general). The preset
supplies the default filming requirements, the Chinese-to-English lexicon for
the content points, and the fallback wording used when a configured phrase has
no translation. Set the category on the campaign; it is otherwise inferred from
the product name.

Never let a Chinese content point disappear from an English message. If it
cannot be translated, the category's fallback content points are sent instead.

## Steam Grooming Brush Brief

Worked example for the pet category. Other categories follow the same shape
with their own preset.

Video requirements:

- 2 videos per creator.
- Each video should be 60 seconds or longer.
- Must tag the brand account.
- Must add the TikTok Shop product link.

Video 1 direction: real review style. Show the pet shedding problem or loose hair collected, the mist feature, a real grooming scene, the pet's real reaction, loose hair removed, and quick cleanup.

Video 2 direction: daily pet-care routine style. Show a real home setting, pet-care routine moment, mist feature, comfortable pet reaction, loose hair removed, and easy cleanup.

Content priority:

1. Loose hair removed.
2. Mist feature.
3. Pet's real reaction.
4. Natural daily pet-care scene.
5. Easy cleanup.

Creators should avoid only filming unboxing, only filming the product on a table, skipping pet usage, hiding the mist feature, skipping the loose hair result, making the video feel like a hard ad, forgetting to tag the brand account, or forgetting the product link.

## Follow-up Scenarios

- First Outreach: not contacted yet, no prior collaboration.
- Re-engagement Outreach: not contacted this round, but a past round or a completed collaboration exists.
- TCM Follow-up: creator source is TCM and no sample activity yet.
- Light Follow-up: contacted 2 days ago, no reply, no sample sent.
- Sample Request Reminder: invited, sample not requested yet.
- Sample Request Confirmation: sample requested, awaiting approval or shipping.
- Collaboration Details Confirmation: creator-orders campaign, creator has replied, quantity and timing not yet agreed.
- Order Instructions: creator-orders campaign, details agreed, order not yet placed.
- Order Number Reminder: order instructions sent, no order number received.
- Address Confirmation: brand-ships campaign blocked on missing or unusable shipping details.
- Sample In Transit Reminder: shipped, not delivered.
- Logistics Exception Confirmation: in transit longer than expected, or delivery risk flagged.
- Sample Delivered Follow-up: sample delivered, no video posted yet.
- Second Follow-up: already followed up, creator still has not replied.
- Partial Video Completion Follow-up: one video posted, more required.
- Needs Revision Reminder: video posted but does not meet the brief.
- Final Follow-up Before Failed Candidate: sample sent or delivered, multiple follow-ups, collaboration may need to be marked failed.
- Completed Thank You: all required videos posted.
- Failed Archive Confirmation: collaboration closed as not completed.
